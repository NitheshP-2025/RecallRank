# Time and Work

**Key points / formula:** Work rate = 1/days to complete alone. Combined rate = sum of individual rates. Time to finish together = 1 / (sum of rates).

**When it's asked (pattern cue):** "A can do a job in X days, B in Y days, working together how long" — including variants with pipes filling/draining a tank (same math, opposite sign for the drain rate).

**Worked micro-example:** A finishes a job in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together?
```
A's rate = 1/10, B's rate = 1/15
Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6
Time together = 6 days
```

**Common gotcha / trick:** Forgetting that a "drain" or "leak" rate is subtracted, not added, when a pipe empties a tank while others fill it; not converting "efficiency ratio" statements (e.g. "A is twice as efficient as B") correctly into rate ratios before combining.
