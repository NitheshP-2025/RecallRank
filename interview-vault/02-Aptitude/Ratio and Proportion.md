# Ratio and Proportion

**Key points / formula:** Ratio a:b compares quantities relatively. Proportion states two ratios are equal (a:b = c:d, so ad = bc). Used to scale quantities up/down proportionally (mixtures, partnerships, map scales).

**When it's asked (pattern cue):** Mixture problems (combining two ratios into one), partnership/profit-sharing based on investment ratio x time, or "if a:b = 2:3, find..." style direct ratio questions.

**Worked micro-example:** Two partners invest in ratio 2:3 for the same duration. Profit of 500 is split how?
```
Total parts = 2+3 = 5
Partner 1 share = (2/5) x 500 = 200
Partner 2 share = (3/5) x 500 = 300
```

**Common gotcha / trick:** In partnership problems, forgetting to multiply investment by time invested when durations differ (profit ratio = investment x time, not investment alone); cross-multiplying incorrectly when solving a:b = c:d for an unknown.
